Songlin Zhao

Computer Science PhD researcher at Lehigh University, working on multimodal foundation models and research agents.

Research

I study multimodal foundation models for medical images and text, and build research agents that connect models with usable workflows. My work spans volumetric segmentation, neuroimaging, and clinical research at Lehigh University and Mayo Clinic.
