BrainNet

A benchmark and toolkit for functional brain network construction and analysis.

fMRIGraph learningBenchmarking
Toolkit + ongoing research

Research question

How much does the choice of connectivity construction change the conclusions of a brain network study?

Approach

BrainNet studies how connectivity construction affects downstream analysis. The toolkit supports systematic comparisons of brain-network methods, with common evaluation settings and reproducible pipelines.

My contribution

First-author research and development of benchmarking workflows for brain network analysis.
