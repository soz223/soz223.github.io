Research question

How can multimodal reference models reveal meaningful individual differences in the brain?

Approach

My work examines how multimodal neuroimaging can describe variation in brain development and disease. The central objective is a reliable reference distribution and meaningful individual deviation, with downstream clinical validation.

My contribution

Research on normative modeling, multimodal representation learning and individualized biomarkers.
