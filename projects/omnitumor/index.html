OmniTumor

Text-prompted volumetric tumor and lesion segmentation across medical imaging modalities.

3D imagingVision-language modelsSegmentation
Research in progress

Research question

How can one model connect textual descriptions with tumors and lesions in three-dimensional images?

Approach

This work brings natural-language prompts into volumetric segmentation. The research connects 3D CT, MRI and CBCT processing with multimodal alignment and clinically relevant evaluation.

My contribution

First-author research on 3D vision-language modeling, data preparation, training and evaluation.
