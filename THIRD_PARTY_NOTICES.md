# Third-party artwork

The Mayo Clinic three-shield mark within `assets/research-loop.svg` belongs to Mayo Clinic.

- Source: [Mayo Clinic logo on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mayo_Clinic_logo.svg)
- Original source cited by that file: [Mayo Clinic](https://www.mayoclinic.org/documents/mc2045-pdf/doc-20078949)
- The shield geometry and original color are preserved. The separate text label follows the illustration's typography.

The surrounding illustration depicts personal research experience and a clinical collaboration setting. The illustrated clinician represents a clinical colleague.
